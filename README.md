# Testosterone and Height

An interactive one-page guide to how testosterone affects growth at each stage of life, and why it speeds up the teen growth spurt without adding to adult height.

## What's on the page

- **Stage explorer.** Six stages, from childhood to adulthood, including teen steroid use and medical treatment for delayed puberty. Each stage shows what testosterone does, its effect on height, a relative growth-speed bar, and a bone diagram with the growth plates open, closing, or closed.
- **The pathway in five steps.** How testosterone rises, how part of it becomes estrogen, and how estrogen eventually closes the growth plates.
- **Myth or fact.** Five quick statements with instant explanations and a final score.

## Key facts

- Testosterone speeds up growth during puberty but doesn't raise final adult height.
- Boys convert part of their testosterone into estrogen, and estrogen is the main signal that closes the growth plates.
- Anabolic steroids can cause growth plates to close early, which can leave teens permanently shorter.
- Short, supervised testosterone treatment for delayed puberty changes the timing of growth, not the final height.
- Once growth plates have fused, testosterone therapy can't add height.
- About 80% of the variation in adult height comes from inherited DNA.

## Accessibility

The stage explorer follows the ARIA tabs pattern with arrow-key, Home, and End navigation. Quiz feedback is announced to screen readers, and animations respect the reduced-motion setting.
